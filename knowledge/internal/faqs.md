# Internal FAQs

## General
**Q: What are the working hours?**
A: Core hours are 10 AM - 4 PM. Flexible schedule outside core hours.

**Q: How do I request time off?**
A: Submit requests through HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance.

**Q: What's the expense policy?**
A: Expenses under $50 don't need pre-approval. Over $50 requires manager approval.

## Engineering
**Q: How do I get access to production?**
A: Complete security training, then submit access request through IT portal.

**Q: What's the deployment process?**
A: All deployments go through CI/CD pipeline. Requires code review approval.

**Q: How do I report a bug?**
A: Create ticket in Jira with severity level and reproduction steps.

## Tools
**Q: How do I reset my password?**
A: Use the "Forgot Password" link on login page or contact IT.

**Q: Where can I find documentation?**
A: Check Confluence or ask in #documentation Slack channel.

**Q: How do I request new software?**
A: Submit request through IT portal with justification.
