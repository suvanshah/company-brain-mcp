# AI Deployment Policy

## Overview
This policy governs the deployment of AI systems within the company infrastructure.

## Approval Process
All AI deployments require:
1. Technical review by the AI Safety team
2. Security assessment
3. Legal compliance check
4. Executive sign-off for production deployments

## Requirements
- All models must be tested for bias and safety
- Deployment must include monitoring and rollback procedures
- Data privacy must be maintained at all times
- Regular audits required every 6 months

## Restrictions
- No deployment of unverified third-party models
- No access to PII without explicit consent
- No autonomous decision-making in critical systems without human oversight
